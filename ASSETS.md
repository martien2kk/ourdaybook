# Asset provenance

All distributed backgrounds, app images and SVG illustrations are generated by `scripts/generate-public-assets.ps1` from colours and geometric primitives. Navigation icons are drawn by `scripts/generate-tab-icons.ps1`. These scripts and their output are covered by this project's PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0; see LICENSE.

No original application raster assets, photographs, screenshots or marketing materials are included. Background filenames are retained to preserve application references and tests; the public artwork depicts abstract circles rather than the animals or scenes described by legacy filenames.

`docs/images/mini-program-code.png` is the owner-provided WeChat mini program code, included at the owner's request so readers can open OUR DAYBOOK. It is separate from the generated demo artwork and is intended for accessing the app, rather than reuse as another app's branding or access code.
