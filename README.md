**One prompt, two photos, one shared day.**

OUR DAYBOOK is a WeChat mini program for capturing everyday moments with a photo and a few words. Whether you are in a long-distance relationship, sharing daily life with a friend, or keeping a journal on your own, it gives those small moments a place to stay.

### What can you do with OUR DAYBOOK?

- **Capture your day**: Follow a daily prompt and photograph the sky, your lunch, your mood, or something that made you pause.
- **Share a space**: Answer the same prompt with a partner. Each person contributes their own photo and words, and both entries become visible once you have both submitted.
- **Keep a personal journal**: Start on your own and record your life without waiting for someone else to join.
- **Revisit your memories**: Browse past entries by date in the memory calendar and watch everyday moments become a story.
- **Create keepsake cards**: Turn entries into minimalist, film-inspired, or scrapbook-style cards, preview them, and save them to your photo album.
- **Make it yours**: Choose an appearance theme, adjust prompt categories, and add your own questions.
- **Plan your next meetup**: Save a date, a city, and a note about what you are looking forward to.

### How to use it

#### 1. Choose how you want to journal

Open the mini program to preview its features, then add your nickname, avatar, and other profile details. Choose a personal space to journal on your own, or a shared space to record daily life with someone else.

#### 2. Invite a partner

One person creates a shared space and sends the invitation code to the other. The other person enters that code to join. You can share a space with a romantic partner or a friend.

#### 3. Capture your side of today

Open the Record tab (「记录」), read the daily prompt, choose a photo, and add a caption of up to 100 characters. A meal, a patch of sunlight, or an ordinary moment is enough. There is no need to stage anything.

#### 4. Unlock the day together

In a shared space, once both people have submitted their entries, open the Together tab (「我们」) to see each other's photos and words. In a personal space, you can complete and revisit entries on your own.

#### 5. Revisit and save

Choose a date in the Calendar tab (「日历」) to return to a past entry. For a day you want to keep close, create a keepsake card, choose your favourite style, and save it.

### About what you share

You can enter your city yourself and control whether it is displayed. OUR DAYBOOK does not require live location tracking. Before uploading a photo or screenshot, cover names, contact details, full addresses, and anything else you do not want to share.

### Ordinary days deserve to be remembered

Not every day needs a big event. What you ate, what caught your eye, and what was on your mind can all become a page you will want to revisit.

**Live your days. Keep the memories together.**

**同一个问题，两张照片，拼成我们共同的一天。**

同日簿是一款微信小程序，用一张照片和一句话，收藏每天的普通瞬间。无论是相隔两地的恋人、想分享日常的朋友，还是独自认真生活的你，都可以在这里慢慢积累属于自己的回忆。

## 在同日簿里，可以做什么？

- **每日记录**：跟随一道生活小题，拍下今天的天空、午餐、心情，或路上让你停留的画面。
- **双人空间**：和另一位伙伴回答同一道问题，各自记录自己的这一半，双方完成后再一起查看照片与文字。
- **个人空间**：一个人也可以开始，记录自己的日常，不必等待另一位伙伴加入。
- **回忆日历**：按日期翻看过去的记录，让零散的生活瞬间慢慢连成故事。
- **纪念卡片**：将记录制作成简约、胶片或手账风格的卡片，预览后保存到相册。
- **个性化记录**：选择喜欢的界面主题、调整题目分类，也可以添加自己的问题。
- **相遇计划**：记下下一次相遇的日期、城市与小小期待。

## 怎么使用？

### 1. 选择你的记录方式

进入小程序后，可以先查看功能预览，再填写昵称、头像等资料。想先记录自己的生活，就从个人空间开始；想和另一位伙伴分享日常，就选择双人空间。

### 2. 邀请一位伙伴

一人创建双人空间，将邀请码分享给另一人；另一人输入邀请码，即可加入同一个空间。伙伴可以是恋人，也可以是想一起记录生活的朋友。

### 3. 留下今天的一半

打开「记录」，查看今日问题，选择一张照片，再写下最多 100 字的文字。不需要精心准备，一顿饭、一束光、一个普通的瞬间，都值得被收藏。

### 4. 一起解锁今天

在双人空间中，双方分别完成当天的提交后，就可以在「我们」里查看彼此的照片与文字。个人空间则可以独立完成记录和回顾。

### 5. 回看与收藏

在「日历」中选择日期，重温已经留下的记录。遇到想特别收藏的一天，可以制作纪念卡片，选择喜欢的风格并保存。

## 关于你的分享

你可以自行填写城市，并控制城市是否展示。同日簿不需要实时定位。上传照片或截图前，请先遮挡姓名、联系方式、详细地址及其他不想分享的信息。

## 让普通的今天，也有被记住的理由

不必每天都有大事发生。今天吃了什么、看见了什么、在想些什么，都可以成为未来想翻看的一页。

**各自生活，共同珍藏。**
